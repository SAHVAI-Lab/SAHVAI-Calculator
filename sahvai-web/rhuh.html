RHUH SAH dataset in the browser

Open non-contrast CT scans of aneurysmal SAH with expert bleeding masks from Río Hortega University Hospital, Valladolid (Cepeda & García-García, Zenodo 2023). Load a case and its mask, run SAHVAI (BrainHemoAI), CLAIM or BLAST-CT on it and compare with the expert. Everything runs on this computer.

Research and education use only

A research demonstration, not a medical device. This model is not validated or approved for clinical use here; do not use it for diagnosis, treatment or any other clinical decision. Segmentations can be wrong; always review them against the images.

Your images stay on this computer

Files are read and processed inside this browser tab on your GPU or CPU. No image data or results are uploaded. Only the page, the model files and the demo cases are downloaded, and the model is cached for later visits.

What you need

  • A recent desktop Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari may work on the CPU).
  • 8 GB RAM or more. WebGPU is fastest; on the CPU a scan takes one to several minutes.
  • A non-contrast axial head CT in Hounsfield units.

RHUH SAH dataset Cepeda & García-García · Valladolid, Spain

Open non-contrast CT scans of aneurysmal SAH with expert manual bleeding masks. Load one case and its mask, run any of the three models on it and compare with the expert segmentation.

Río Hortega University Hospital · Zenodo
  1. Download subarachnoid_hemorrhage_rhuh.rar (649 MB) from Zenodo record 8228847 and extract it (browsers cannot open RAR archives).
  2. Drop the two NIfTI files of one case here (the CT and its mask), or choose them below.
  3. Pick a model and press Run. The expert mask is drawn in white and Dice and volumes are compared as "any blood".

Drop one RHUH case here (CT + expert mask, .nii / .nii.gz)

CT: none · mask: none

The dataset description lists anonymisation, resampling, skull stripping and intensity normalisation among its preprocessing. The three models expect raw Hounsfield units and the full head, so the page checks the intensity range and warns when a volume does not look like HU; skull-stripped input can also affect the brain extraction of SAHVAI (BrainHemoAI).
