DeepBleed ICH in the browser

The open-source 3D network for intracerebral hemorrhage from the MISTIE trial investigators at Johns Hopkins (Sharrock et al., Neuroinformatics 2021): ICH and IVH segmentation and volume on non-contrast head CT. Everything runs on this computer.

Research and education use only

A research demonstration, not a medical device. This model is not validated or approved for clinical use here; do not use it for diagnosis, treatment or any other clinical decision. Segmentations can be wrong; always review them against the images.

Your images stay on this computer

Files are read and processed inside this browser tab on your GPU or CPU. No image data or results are uploaded. Only the page, the model files and the demo cases are downloaded, and the model is cached for later visits.

What you need

  • A recent desktop Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari may work on the CPU).
  • 8 GB RAM or more. WebGPU is fastest; on the CPU a scan takes one to several minutes.
  • A non-contrast axial head CT in Hounsfield units.

DeepBleed MISTIE trial investigators · 3D V-Net for ICH

Intracerebral and intraventricular hemorrhage (one combined class) on non-contrast head CT, with volume: the first open-source 3D network for ICH segmentation, trained on MISTIE II and validated on MISTIE III (Dice 0.91).

Sharrock, Hanley, Muschelli et al. · Johns Hopkins (BIOS)
90.2 MB model download, cached after the first run.
Not the same task as SAHVAI or CLAIM: DeepBleed was trained on spontaneous ICH with or without IVH (MISTIE II) and labels ICH and IVH as one class; subarachnoid or subdural blood is not its target. The brain is extracted with SAHVAI's method (DeepBleed uses FSL BET, which cannot run in a browser); tick Brain outline in the viewer to check it.

Drop a head CT here

A zip of a DICOM study, a DICOM folder, loose DICOM files, a .nii / .nii.gz or a .nrrd volume. The brain is aligned to DeepBleed’s 1.5 mm, 128³ CT template, segmented there and mapped back to the scan.

How it runs here. As DeepBleed’s predict.py: brain-only image (outside 0) → rigid registration to the template (centres of mass, then mutual information, as ANTs) → linear resampling to 128³ at 1.5 mm → V-Net → mask > 0.5 → mapped back to the scan (linear interpolation of the mask ≥ 0.5, as ANTs’ genericLabel). It reproduces a quirk of the original that the network was trained with: the scan reaches ANTs turned 180° about the vertical axis. With the same brain mask, this port agrees with the original pipeline at Dice 0.93 (15.2 vs 15.1 mL) on the MISTIE demo; differences come from the registration and, without that mask, from the brain extraction.
