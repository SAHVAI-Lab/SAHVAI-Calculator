SAHVAI Web models in the browser

Drop a non-contrast head CT into any panel to segment hemorrhage and measure volumes with one of three published models, or load a case from the open RHUH dataset and compare a model with the expert mask. Everything runs on this computer.

1 SAHVAI2 CLAIM3 BLAST-CT4 RHUH dataset

Research and education use only

A research demonstration, not a medical device. None of these models is validated or approved for clinical use here; do not use them for diagnosis, treatment or any other clinical decision. Segmentations can be wrong; always review them against the images.

Your images stay on this computer

Files are read and processed inside this browser tab on your GPU or CPU. No image data or results are uploaded. Only the page, the model files and the demo cases are downloaded, and the models are cached for later visits.

What you need

  • A recent desktop Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari may work on the CPU).
  • 8 GB RAM or more. WebGPU is fastest; on the CPU a scan takes one to several minutes.
  • A non-contrast axial head CT in Hounsfield units.

SAHVAI Mayo · BrainHemoAI Hybrid 2D/3D UNet

Subarachnoid (SAH), intraparenchymal (IPH) and intraventricular (IVH) hemorrhage on non-contrast head CT, with volumes.

Hu et al. · Shanghai / BrainHemoAI
Head alignment °
Model download about 26 MB, cached after the first visit.

Drop a head CT here

A zip of a DICOM study, a DICOM folder, loose DICOM files, a .nii / .nii.gz or a .nrrd volume. From a study, the axial non-contrast head series nearest 5 mm is used (bone-kernel, contrast and reformatted series are skipped). Thin slices are averaged to 5 mm.

reference: none · must share the scan's geometry; Dice is reported per class

Citation, licence and disclaimer

Model. Hu P, Zhou H, Yan T, et al. Deep learning-assisted identification and quantification of aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage in non-contrast CT scans: development and external validation of Hybrid 2D/3D UNet. NeuroImage 2023;279:120321. doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2023.120321

Code and weights. BrainHemoAI (Dr Ping Hu and colleagues), converted to ONNX for the browser. Used with the permission of Dr Ping Hu under the MIT License. Demo case: head.nrrd from the BrainHemoAI repository.

How it runs here. Reorientation and resampling to 0.5 × 0.5 × 5 mm, brain extraction, midline alignment, five HU windows, 18-slice windows through the network (split into nine ONNX parts so it fits in a browser tab), post-processing and voxel-count volumes, as in the original code. On the BrainHemoAI demo case the port matches the PyTorch pipeline to within 6 voxels.

CLAIM aSAH Charité Berlin · nnU-Net multiclass

Six classes on non-contrast CT after aneurysmal SAH: SAH, intraventricular hemorrhage, ventricles, intracerebral hemorrhage, aneurysm and subdural hematoma.

CLAIM · Charité Lab for AI in Medicine, Berlin
CLAIM weights not installed yet. The converted model files (models/claim/) are added once the CC BY-NC 4.0 weights from Zenodo have been converted and checked against the authors' reference output. Until then this panel cannot run.
Inference
2D model, about 93 MB download, cached.

Drop a head CT here

A zip of a DICOM study, a DICOM folder, loose DICOM files, a .nii / .nii.gz or a .nrrd volume. The 2D model works slice by slice at 0.445 mm in plane; it was trained on 5.5 to 6.5 mm slices, so thin slices are averaged to 5 mm. The demo cases include the expert labels, so Dice is shown.

reference: none · must share the scan's geometry; Dice is reported per class

Citation, licence and disclaimer

Model. Kiewitz J, Aydin OU, Hilbert A, Gultom M, Nouri A, Khalil AA, Vajkoczy P, Tanioka S, Ishida F, Dengler NF, Frey D. Deep learning-based multiclass segmentation in aneurysmal subarachnoid hemorrhage. Front Neurol 2024;15:1490216. doi:10.3389/fneur.2024.1490216 (preprint: medRxiv 2024.06.24.24309431)

Code and weights. CLAIM project page · github.com/claim-berlin/aSAH-multiclass-segmentation · weights on Zenodo doi:10.5281/zenodo.12579491. Licensed CC BY-NC 4.0 (attribution, non-commercial use only); the nnU-Net 2D network is converted to ONNX without changes to the weights. nnU-Net: Isensee F, Jaeger PF, Kohl SAA, Petersen J, Maier-Hein KH. Nat Methods 2021;18:203–211 (Apache-2.0).

Demo data. Three cases from the BHSD dataset as distributed in the CLAIM repository: Wu B, Xie Y, Zhang Z, et al. BHSD: a 3D multi-class brain hemorrhage segmentation dataset. In: Machine Learning in Medical Imaging (MLMI 2023), LNCS 14348. Springer; 2024. doi:10.1007/978-3-031-45673-2_15. The reference labels shown with the demo are those supplied with the CLAIM demo data.

How it runs here. As nnUNetv2_predict for the 2D configuration: in-plane resampling to 0.4453 mm (linear here, cubic spline in nnU-Net), CT normalisation (clip to −3…88 HU, z-score with mean 32.47, SD 27.41), 512 × 512 tiles, argmax. The authors report similar accuracy for their 2D and 3D models and used the 2D model for external validation.

BLAST-CT Cambridge & Imperial · traumatic brain injury lesions

Four lesion classes from the CENTER-TBI work: intraparenchymal haemorrhage, extra-axial haemorrhage, perilesional oedema and intraventricular haemorrhage. Use it for SAH after a fall (traumatic SAH is part of the extra-axial class) or for traumatic ICH.

Menon, Newcombe, Glocker et al. · University of Cambridge NeuroICU · Imperial College London
Models
About 2 MB per model, cached.
Not the same task as panels 1 and 2: EAH combines subarachnoid, subdural and extradural blood, and the model was trained on traumatic brain injury, not aneurysmal SAH.

Drop a head CT here

A zip of a DICOM study, a DICOM folder, loose DICOM files, a .nii / .nii.gz or a .nrrd volume. The scan is resampled to 1 mm isotropic, as in the blast-ct tool.

reference: none · must share the scan's geometry; Dice is reported per class

Citation, licence and disclaimer

Model. Monteiro M, Newcombe VFJ, Mathieu F, Adatia K, Kamnitsas K, Ferrante E, Das T, Whitehouse D, Rueckert D, Menon DK, Glocker B. Multiclass semantic segmentation and quantification of traumatic brain injury lesions on head CT using deep learning: an algorithm development and multicentre validation study. Lancet Digit Health 2020;2(6):e314–e322. doi:10.1016/S2589-7500(20)30085-6

Code and weights. github.com/biomedia-mira/blast-ct (BioMedIA, Imperial College London, with the University of Cambridge Division of Anaesthesia), Apache License 2.0, Copyright 2020 Imperial College London. The 12 pre-trained DeepMedic models are converted to ONNX (fp16) without retraining. Architecture: Kamnitsas K, Ledig C, Newcombe VFJ, et al. Med Image Anal 2017;36:61–78. The software "is not intended for clinical use" (blast-ct README). Demo: scan_0 and its label map from the blast-ct repository.

How it runs here. As blast-ct: reorientation, 1 mm isotropic linear resampling (SimpleITK-style), window −15 to 100 HU scaled to −1…1, three-pathway DeepMedic over tiles, softmax averaged over the chosen models, argmax. Output tiles of 60³ give the same labels as the tool's 30³ tiles (checked: 0 voxels differ).

RHUH SAH dataset Cepeda & García-García · Valladolid, Spain

Open non-contrast CT scans of aneurysmal SAH with expert manual bleeding masks. Load one case and its mask, run any of the three models on it and compare with the expert segmentation.

Río Hortega University Hospital · Zenodo
  1. Download subarachnoid_hemorrhage_rhuh.rar (649 MB) from Zenodo record 8228847 and extract it (browsers cannot open RAR archives).
  2. Drop the two NIfTI files of one case here (the CT and its mask), or choose them below.
  3. Pick a model and press Run. The expert mask is drawn in white and Dice and volumes are compared as "any blood".

Drop one RHUH case here (CT + expert mask, .nii / .nii.gz)

CT: none · mask: none

The dataset description lists anonymisation, resampling, skull stripping and intensity normalisation among its preprocessing. The three models expect raw Hounsfield units and the full head, so the page checks the intensity range and warns when a volume does not look like HU; skull-stripped input can also affect the brain extraction in panel 1.
